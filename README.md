# Momo Dog (摸摸狗) — pet a sleeping dog before bed, a calming iPhone app

Momo Dog is a calming iPhone app for winding down before sleep. Stroke a sleeping dog with your finger: the fur bends under your touch and springs back, its breathing slows, and it drifts into a deep sleep. No scores, tasks or feeding, and no account needed.

[Website](https://sona-orbit.github.io/momo-dog/) · [How to use](https://sona-orbit.github.io/momo-dog/guide/) · [Privacy](https://sona-orbit.github.io/momo-dog/privacy/)

## Features

- **Fur that really moves**: stroke with the grain and it lies down toward your finger, then slowly springs back; stroke against it and it ruffles up. Curls bounce, long hair drifts after your hand.
- **20 dogs, 7 kinds of fur**: curly, short, double coat, stand-off, wiry, long and silky. The black poodle, latte shorthair, Shiba Inu, Samoyed and golden retriever are free.
- **Nothing on screen while you pet**: rest your finger for 0.8 seconds and a menu opens by your thumb.
- **Bedtime**: after about a minute of slow petting the dog sleeps deeply and the screen dims; a sleep timer fades the breathing and turns the screen black.
- **Faint breathing sound and gentle haptics**, both can be turned off.
- **Free with ads** outside the petting screen; a one-time membership unlocks all 20 dogs and removes ads. iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
