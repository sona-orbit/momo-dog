How to use

How to pet

Stroke slowly from head to tail, which is with the grain. About 3 cm per second feels best. Too fast or too hard and the dog goes from deep sleep to light sleep, but it never wakes up upset. Stroking against the grain is fine too; the fur ruffles up for a while.

Opening the menu

Keep your finger still on the dog for 0.8 seconds. Five buttons open beside your thumb: sleep timer, switch dog, breathing sound, haptics, settings. Without lifting your finger, slide onto the one you want and let go. On the sleep timer or breathing sound, nudge left or right to pick a value. Letting go over empty space cancels.

Switching dogs

Switch dog opens a row of feel cards. Swipe left and right; the fur types at the bottom jump to a group. Tap a card to choose that dog.

Locked dogs

The free version has 5 dogs. Tap a locked dog to pet it for 20 seconds, then the unlock page appears. There you can also watch an ad to pet that dog for the rest of the day.

Sleep timer

In the menu, slide to the moon and pick 3, 5, 10 or 15 minutes. When time is up the breathing fades out over 6 seconds and the screen goes black. Tap once to return.

Membership and restoring

Membership is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. On a new phone or after reinstalling, tap Restore purchase under Membership in settings, using the same Apple Account.

Why is it so quiet?

Momo Dog keeps only very faint breathing on purpose. You can set it to Soft or turn it off in settings. With your iPhone on silent there is no sound.

Problems

In settings, tap Report a problem, or email sona.orbit.hi@gmail.com.
