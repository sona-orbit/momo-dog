Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

No account

Momo Dog needs no sign-up. Your settings, which dogs you have petted and how many nights you stayed until it fell asleep are stored only on your iPhone. We can't see them.

Purchases

Membership is paid through Apple's App Store. We never receive your payment details.

Ads

The free version uses Google AdMob to show ads at the bottom of the dog picker and settings, plus optional video ads you choose to watch. To show and measure ads, Google may receive your IP address, device type and app usage. Momo Dog does not ask for permission to track and does not use the advertising identifier to track you across apps. Where the law requires consent, you are asked the first time you open the app and can change it later under Ad privacy choices in settings. Members never load ads. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Reporting a problem

Only when you tap Send: what you wrote, an optional email, the app version, system version, device model, language, and a random ID created at install (used only to limit how often you can send). This is stored in a Google Sheet owned by Sona Orbit and used only to handle the problem.

This website

This website uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. It is about the website only, not the app.

Contact

For any privacy question, email sona.orbit.hi@gmail.com.
